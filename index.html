      Three ideas to know first

      Almost everything in ComicPad follows from these three ideas. Once they click, the rest of the studio feels familiar.

      Your wallet is your studio key

      A wallet is a secure account that proves you own your work and receives your payments. It is how ComicPad knows you are you, so there is no password to create or forget. Quiva works with Hedera-compatible wallets such as HashPack and Blade.

      Everything starts with a Collection

      A Collection is your comic or manga title, like Galactic Ronin. It holds all of your Episodes. Even if you only have one chapter, it lives inside a Collection.

      Some actions are permanent, and we always warn you first

      Drafts are flexible. Publishing on-chain, listing on the marketplace, and publishing a drop are permanent. Before each one you will see a plain-language warning and be asked to tick a box and confirm. Nothing permanent ever happens by accident.

      The five words you'll see

      Quiva uses five words very deliberately. Each one means exactly one thing, everywhere in the product.

      WordWhat it meansWho does it
      CreateBuild your content inside ComicPad. This happens off-chain, so it is private to you and fully editable.You
      Publish (on-chain)Record your ownership and story details permanently on Hedera, the network Quiva is built on.You
      ListPut a published episode on the marketplace for sale at a fixed price, available any time.You
      DropA sale campaign with a schedule or rules, such as a whitelist, a countdown, or a falling price.You
      MintThe moment a reader buys a copy and receives it in their wallet.Your readers

      Tip. Creators never "mint to the marketplace." You publish, then you list or create a drop. Your readers mint when they buy.

      Step 3: Publish on-chain

      This is the moment your comic becomes permanently, verifiably yours.

      What "on-chain" means, in plain words

      Think of it as registering your work with a public, tamper-proof record book that no company can edit or erase, including Quiva. That record book is Hedera, a public network. Publishing writes your Collection and Episode details there, stamped with the exact date and time, and assigns ownership to your wallet.

      Your page files are stored separately on IPFS through Pinata, a network of storage servers spread around the world. This means your comic doesn't depend on any single Quiva server, and every file has a unique fingerprint, so any tampering would be detected.

      1. Open your Episode and select Publish On-Chain.
      2. Read the warning and tick the confirmation box.

        The button stays disabled until you do.

      3. Confirm in your wallet.

        Your wallet may ask you to approve the transaction. Hedera network fees are very small.

      4. Done.

        Your Collection and Episode metadata are on Hedera, ownership is assigned to your wallet, and the Episode appears in your ComicPad Library.

      This action is permanent

      You are about to publish this Episode to Hedera. This action is permanent and cannot be reversed.

      Publishing on-chain does not put your Episode on sale. After publishing, you still need to List it or create a Drop.

      Step 4: Choose how to sell

      Once published, an Episode can be sold in two ways. Both lead to the same result: readers mint copies, you earn from every primary sale, and your copies can be traded on the secondary market where you keep earning royalties.

      List on the marketplace

      Always available, at a fixed price. Best for steady, ongoing sales of a back catalogue or a chapter you want to keep available.

      You set: rarity, supply, price, and max per wallet.

      Create a drop

      A sale with a schedule or rules. Best for launches where you want hype, scarcity, and momentum.

      You set: everything above, plus a sale type and timing.

      Rarity tiers: how many copies can exist

      Every Episode can be sold in up to three tiers, called rarities. Rarity controls the maximum number of copies, not the price. You choose the price yourself.

      RarityCopies you can createBest for
      Legendary1 to 5,000Your most collectible edition. Highest scarcity, and copies are serial-numbered.
      Epic1 to 8,000Dedicated fans and collectors.
      CommonUnlimitedMaximum reach. Anyone can own it.
      Supply limits are permanent

      Once a listing or drop is published, its supply cannot be changed. This is the promise your collectors rely on, so choose your numbers carefully.

      Each rarity is set up separately. If you want to sell an Episode as both Legendary and Common, you create one listing or drop for each.

      Option A: List on the marketplace

      Use this to sell an Episode directly, without running a campaign. As soon as you list, readers can mint instantly.

      1. Open a published Episode and select List on Marketplace.
      2. Choose the rarity.

        Legendary, Epic, or Common.

      3. Set the total supply.

        Stay within the limit for your chosen rarity.

      4. Set the price in HBAR.

        HBAR is the digital currency used on Hedera. The price shown to readers is per copy.

      5. Set the maximum per wallet.

        This stops one person from buying up all your copies.

      6. Review, tick the confirmation, and list.

        Once listed, the item becomes tradable and can't be undone.

      What happens next

      • Your Episode becomes purchasable straight away.
      • Readers can mint instantly.
      • Secondary trading is enabled, so you begin earning royalties on resales.

      Option B: Create a drop

      A drop is a sale event with its own page, countdown, and rules. You build it in six short steps.

      E.0 Choose the Episode and rarity

      Pick the Episode from your list, then choose Legendary, Epic, or Common. Each rarity is its own drop.

      E.1 Set the sale details

      • Supply. How many copies this drop offers, for example 5,000.
      • Price. The cost per copy in HBAR, for example 12 HBAR.
      • Max per wallet. For example, 3 copies per wallet.
      • Drop banner. The image shown on your drop card and in promotions.

      E.2 Choose a sale type

      Sale typeHow it worksGood for
      PublicOpen to everyone. First come, first served.A simple, wide launch.
      WhitelistOnly wallets on your approved list can mint. Think of it as a guest list.Rewarding your most loyal fans.
      FCFS (whitelist, then public)Whitelisted wallets get early access. When that window ends, the drop opens to everyone automatically.Priority for fans without excluding newcomers.
      Dutch auctionThe price starts high and falls over time. You set the start price, end price, how much it drops, and how often.Letting the market find the right price.

      E.3 Add your whitelist (only if you chose Whitelist or FCFS)

      1. Upload a file or paste wallet addresses.

        Upload a .csv, .txt, or .json file with one wallet per line, or paste addresses into the text box. Both Hedera addresses (like 0.0.12345) and EVM addresses (like 0xabc…) are accepted, and EVM addresses convert automatically.

      2. Check the automatic clean-up.

        ComicPad removes duplicates and flags invalid entries. You will see a summary such as: Valid wallets 1,248. Duplicates removed 17. Invalid addresses 4. Select the invalid count to review each problem line.

      3. Set your allocation rules.

        Choose a max per wallet for whitelisted buyers (for example, 2). You can also reserve part of the supply for them (for example, 500 copies), with the rest released to the public after the whitelist window closes.

      4. Select Save Whitelist.

        This locks the list to your drop.

      E.4 Set the schedule

      Choose a start date and time and an end date and time, for example Dec 14 at 4:00 PM UTC to Dec 16 at 4:00 PM UTC. Your time zone is detected automatically so what you see is accurate. Once published, the schedule is enforced automatically, and no one, including you, needs to press a button to open or close the sale.

      E.5 Review and publish

      Check the summary carefully: Episode and rarity, supply, price, max per wallet, whitelist details, and your banner preview. Then select Publish Drop.

      Get paid: sales and royalties

      You earn in two ways, and the second one is what makes Quiva different.

      Primary sales

      Every time a reader mints a copy from your listing or drop, you are paid. The split between you and any collaborators happens automatically, in the same moment as the sale.

      Secondary royalties

      Every time a collector resells one of your copies, you receive a percentage of that sale. This continues for as long as your work is traded.

      How royalties reach you

      You choose your royalty percentage during setup, and it locks permanently when you publish. From then on, the Hedera network itself collects and delivers it. Quiva doesn't hold your money and you never need to claim it.

      • Automatic. Your royalty arrives in your wallet at the moment of each resale.
      • Hard to bypass. The royalty applies to every transfer of your token, including sales made outside Quiva. Even a gift transfer with no payment triggers a small fallback fee.
      • Permanent. No marketplace can switch it off.

      Worked example

      A collector resells one of your copies for 100 HBAR. Your royalty is set at 5%, and the marketplace fee is 2.5%.

      WhoReceives
      You, the creator5 HBAR, instantly
      Quiva marketplace fee2.5 HBAR
      The seller92.5 HBAR

      Fees are always shown before you confirm any action.

      What owning a Quiva comic means

      On most comic apps, what you buy is a licence to read, and it can vanish if the platform changes or closes. On Quiva, you own a copy.

      On most platforms

      • You rent access to read
      • You can't resell or transfer
      • The creator earns nothing when readers trade
      • Your library depends on the platform surviving

      On Quiva

      • Your copy lives in your own wallet
      • You can keep it, gift it, or resell it
      • The creator earns a royalty on every resale
      • Your copy is stored on a public network, not only on Quiva's servers

      What does "mint" mean? Minting is our word for buying a fresh copy directly from the creator. When you mint, a new numbered copy is created and delivered to your wallet.

      Before you begin

      Two things to have ready, and one you can do in a minute.

      A wallet

      A wallet is your personal, secure account for holding your comics and your money. Quiva works with Hedera wallets such as HashPack and Blade. Your wallet gives you a private recovery phrase, a list of words that can restore your account. Keep it offline and never share it with anyone.

      Some HBAR

      HBAR is the digital currency of Hedera. Comic prices on Quiva are shown in HBAR. Have enough in your wallet to cover the price of your copy, plus a very small network fee for processing the purchase.

      A profile (worth 10 XP)

      Your profile is your identity on Quiva. Setting it up early also earns you your first XP, which counts toward future rewards. See "Earn XP while you collect" below.

      Keep your recovery phrase private

      Quiva will never ask for your recovery phrase. Anyone who does is trying to steal from you.

      Connect your wallet and set up your profile

      1. Select Connect Wallet.

        You'll find it at the top of the marketplace.

      2. Choose your wallet and approve.

        Approving only shares your public address with Quiva. It doesn't spend anything.

      3. Complete your profile.

        If you see a "Profile Incomplete" badge on your snapshot card, select Complete Profile.

      4. Check your snapshot.

        Your XP balance, your rank on the leaderboard, and quick buttons to open your XP Dashboard or Referrals are all in one place.

      If you see "Wrong network detected. Switch to Hedera to continue," open your wallet's network settings and switch to Hedera.

      Buying a copy

      Choose a listing, check the price and rarity, select buy, and approve in your wallet. The copy moves to your wallet within seconds.

      Selling a copy

      Choose the item from your collection, set your asking price in HBAR, and confirm in your wallet. When someone buys it, the payment arrives in your wallet.

      Where the money goes

      Creators are paid a royalty on every resale. It's collected automatically by the Hedera network at the moment of sale. For example, on a 100 HBAR sale with a 5% creator royalty and a 2.5% marketplace fee:

      WhoReceives
      Creator5 HBAR
      Quiva marketplace fee2.5 HBAR
      Seller92.5 HBAR

      The creator's royalty percentage is shown on each drop page, and fees are shown before you confirm.

      Leaderboards and badges

      The Global leaderboard ranks everyone by XP and highlights your own row. The Friends leaderboard shows people you referred. As you go, you can unlock badges such as Bronze Reader, Silver Collector, Gold Referrer, and Super Creator, with progress bars showing what's next.

      What XP is for

      • At mainnet launch, your XP converts to Q Points at a one-to-one rate.
      • Q Points can be used toward mint discounts and to unlock exclusive content or early releases. They cover part of a price, and the rest is paid in HBAR.
      • Q Points may expire if left unused for too long, so use them.
      • Your activity on ComicPad and the marketplace also shapes your Q Token airdrop at the Token Generation Event.

      Stay safe

      • Never share your recovery phrase. Not with support, not with a friend, not with a website.
      • Read your wallet pop-up every time. Check the price and what you are approving before you confirm.
      • Look for the Verified badge and check the creator's profile if a collection looks unfamiliar.
      • Use official Quiva links only. Be careful with links sent in direct messages.
      • Spot copied work? Use the Report infringement button on the drop page.
      • Only spend what you're comfortable with. Collectible values can go up and down.

      Plain-language glossary

      Every technical word you might meet on Quiva, explained without jargon.

      Everyday Quiva terms

      Collection
      A full comic or manga title. On other marketplaces this is called a Collection.
      Episode
      A single chapter of a Collection. It is what readers read, mint, collect, and trade.
      Rarity
      An edition tier (Legendary, Epic, or Common) that sets the maximum number of copies that can exist.
      Supply
      The number of copies available in a listing or drop. It is permanent once published.
      Create
      Building content in ComicPad. Private and editable.
      Publish (on-chain)
      Permanently recording your Collection and Episode on Hedera, with ownership assigned to your wallet.
      List
      Putting a published Episode on the marketplace at a fixed price, available any time.
      Drop
      A sale campaign with a schedule or rules, and its own page and countdown.
      Mint
      What a reader does when they buy a fresh copy. A new numbered copy is created for them.
      Whitelist
      A guest list of wallets allowed to mint early or exclusively.
      FCFS
      First come, first served. On Quiva, it usually means whitelisted wallets mint first and then the drop opens to everyone.
      Dutch auction
      A sale where the price starts high and drops in steps until the copies sell.
      Airdrop / Free claim
      A free distribution of copies to a chosen list of wallets.
      Royalty
      A percentage of every resale that goes back to the creator, automatically.
      Floor price
      The lowest price at which a copy is currently listed for sale.
      Serial number
      The unique number of an individual copy, for example 47 of 5,000.
      Primary market
      Where copies are sold for the first time, straight from the creator.
      Secondary market
      Where collectors resell copies to each other.

      Technology terms

      Wallet
      A secure personal account that holds your comics and money and proves who you are. Examples for Hedera include HashPack and Blade.
      Recovery phrase
      The private list of words that can restore your wallet. Never share it.
      Hedera
      The public network Quiva is built on. It records ownership and payments, with fast confirmation and very low fees.
      HBAR
      Hedera's digital currency. Prices on Quiva are shown in HBAR.
      On-chain
      Recorded permanently on a public network rather than only in a company's private database.
      Off-chain
      Kept privately inside ComicPad. Fully editable.
      Network fee
      A very small charge paid to the network for processing a transaction.
      Smart contract
      A small automated program that carries out agreed rules exactly, such as a sale's price, limits, and timing.
      KiloScribe
      The service Quiva uses to record and index comic details on Hedera.
      IPFS and Pinata
      A shared network of storage servers, and the service that keeps your comic files on it, so they don't depend on a single company.
      Testnet
      A practice version of the network used before the real launch.
      Mainnet
      The live, real version of the network.
      XP
      Experience points earned for taking part in Quiva during testnet.
      Q Points
      What XP becomes at mainnet. They can be used toward mint discounts and exclusive content.
      Q Token ($Q)
      Quiva's utility token, used inside the Quiva ecosystem.
